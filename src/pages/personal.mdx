## Ballroom

I am interested in competitive ballroom dancing and compete across the US. I currently serve as the President of the [Ballroom Club at Stanford](https://ballroom.stanford.edu).

My dance partner is [Yijia Shao](https://cs.stanford.edu/~shaoyj/).

You can read more about us in this [feature by Stanford](#placeholder-for-stanford-feature-link).
